President's Message
Welcome to Tanzeem-al-Lissan, Faisalabad, an organization proudly serving individuals with special needs since 1996. What began with just six hearing-impaired students has grown into one of Punjab's leading institutions for special education, rehabilitation, and professional training.

Tanzeem-al-Lissan
A voluntary Organization for Special Children
Tanzeem al-Lissan is a nonprofit charitable organization established in 1996 in Faisalabad. It works for the uplift and welfare of children with special needs in both urban and rural areas, enabling them to lead meaningful, independent lives. Since its inception, Tanzeem al-Lissan has been actively engaged in the rehabilitation of children with disabilities through special education and therapeutic services for the hearing-impaired, intellectually challenged, and physically disabled.
In July 1996, the organization began its journey with the establishment of the Danish Center for Hearing-Impaired Children, starting with only six students in a rented building. As enrollment grew, larger facilities were needed, leading to expansion into bigger campuses. Today, Tanzeem operates from a purpose-built facility with 57 rooms on 8 canals of land, allotted by the district government.
Vision
To empower individuals with special needs through inclusive education, rehabilitation, and professional training, enabling them to live with dignity, independence, and equal opportunities.
Mission
To provide quality special education, rehabilitation, and therapeutic services for hearing-impaired, intellectually challenged, physically challenged, and autistic individuals, while preparing skilled professionals through accredited academic and training programs.
Objectives
* To provide special education and rehabilitation services tailored to the needs of hearing-impaired, intellectually challenged, physically challenged, and autistic individuals.
* To offer therapeutic and clinical support services, including speech and language therapy and psychological services.
* Offering PGD and degree programs in SLT, ASD, Clinical Psychology, Special Education, IT, and more, preparing skilled professionals and empowering students for academic and career success.
* To promote vocational training and life-skills development for independent and productive living.
* To encourage holistic development through co-curricular activities, sports, arts, and social participation.
* To strengthen parental involvement and community awareness in supporting individuals with special needs.
* To expand outreach services to underserved and rural communities.
* To uphold standards of quality, inclusion, professionalism, and compassion in all programs and services.
What We Do ?
Tanzeem-al-Lissan is a leading organization dedicated to the holistic development and empowerment of children with special needs. It provides specialized education, rehabilitation, and therapeutic services for children with hearing impairments, intellectual and developmental disabilities, Autism Spectrum Disorder, Down Syndrome, ADHD, ODD, learning difficulties, physical disabilities, cerebral palsy, speech and language disorders, and emotional or behavioral challenges.
The organization delivers individualized support, skill-based training, and inclusive programs to children from both urban and rural communities, with a strong focus on building confidence, independence, and self-worth. To promote economic self-reliance, Tanzeem-al-Lissan also offers a unique Information Technology degree program exclusively for hearing-impaired students, enabling them to acquire practical, employable skills.
In addition, Tanzeem-al-Lissan contributes to capacity building in the special needs sector by offering Postgraduate Diploma programs in Speech and Language Therapy, Autism Spectrum Disorder, and Clinical Psychology, along with degree programs in Special Education, Computer Science, Psychology, English Linguistic and related disciplines, strengthening professional expertise and service quality.
Why Choose Tanzeem-al-Lissan?
At Tanzeem-al-Lissan, we are committed to delivering high-quality special education and rehabilitation services at the most affordable cost, ensuring that no child is deprived of care due to financial limitations. We firmly believe that every child with special needs has the right to learn, grow, and participate meaningfully in society.
Our programs are designed to prepare students to become confident, capable, and socially integrated individuals. The strong academic reputation of Tanzeem attracts families from diverse backgrounds, with students of Danish Degree College consistently securing excellent results in board and university examinations. Beyond academics, we focus on the overall development and well-being of each child. Through carefully designed Individualized Education Plans (IEPs), we strengthen language, communication, social interaction, cognitive development, motor skills, and self-help abilities, significantly enhancing quality of life.
Our modern educational environment is supported by dedicated, well-trained professionals who regularly participate in workshops and seminars to maintain high standards of care and instruction. At Tanzeem-al-Lissan, quality, compassion, and affordability come together giving every child the opportunity to learn, thrive, and find their rightful place in society.

1996 - Foundation and Early Beginnings

Tanzeem-al-Lissan, a nonprofit charitable organization, was established in 1996 in Faisalabad with the mission of uplifting and empowering children with special needs, both in urban and rural areas. SEHARA Centres (Special Education for Hearing impaired in Accessible Rural Areas)

Vision
To ensure children with hearing impairment in rural and underserved areas have access to quality education and rehabilitation, enabling confident, independent, and dignified lives.
Mission
SEHARA promotes inclusive and quality special education for deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals in rural communities through accessible education, therapeutic support, and skill development programs.
Objectives
* To provide a safe and supportive learning environment that encourages lifelong learning.
* To offer educational programs that support the academic and overall development of hearing-impaired students.
* To deliver learning activities that develop diverse skills and problem-solving abilities.
* To empower students and families with confidence and self-reliance.

The rapid advancement of electronic media has increased awareness among persons with disabilities, even in the remotest areas of the country. Today, individuals no longer seek pity; they demand justice, equal opportunities, and recognition as full members of society.
At Tanzeem-al-Lissan, while focusing on children with hearing impairments in rural areas, we not only provide quality education, therapy, and rehabilitation services, but also work toward their economic and social empowerment, enabling them to live with dignity and become contributing members of their families and communities.
The SEHARA Program is a community-based outreach initiative launched in February 1999 to provide special education for hearing-impaired children in rural areas of Faisalabad. The program was initially strengthened through financial support from the Punjab Welfare Trust for the Disabled (PWTD).
Following a detailed survey, the villages and towns as Ramdewali, Lehlan, Sadhar, Makkuna, Sammundri, Chak Jhumra, Aminpur, Dijkot, Khurrinwala, and Barnala were selected for establishing the first Centres. In August 2001, five additional Centres were established with the assistance of TVO, located in Sangla Hill, Phalor, Shahkot, Satiana, and Tandianwala.
Today, these Centres continue to function with the financial support of PWTD and the active participation of local communities, ensuring sustainable access to education and rehabilitation services for rural hearing-impaired children.

1. Basic Education
Special education is provided from Play Group to Matric, following the Punjab Textbook curriculum, adapted to meet the learning needs of hearing-impaired students.
3. Free Hearing Aids
All hearing-impaired students are provided with hearing aids free of cost, ensuring equal access to essential assistive technology.
4. Fine Arts
Special focus is placed on developing artistic talents, helping students enhance creativity, self-expression, and confidence.
5. Computer Training
Every student receives basic computer education, preparing them to navigate modern technology and digital learning platforms.
6. Vocational Skills Training
In addition to formal education, students are trained in vocational skills by qualified instructors, equipping them with practical skills for future employment and economic independence.
7. Sports and Co-Curricular Activities
Regular sports and co-curricular activities are organized to promote physical fitness, teamwork, and social skills among students.
Parents' Counselling & Mothers' Meetings
Regular counselling sessions keep parents informed about students' academic, behavioral, and developmental progress, with professional guidance for home support. Monthly Mothers' Meetings, held on the first Friday, strengthen parent-institution collaboration.
Free Education
Free education, including textbooks, learning materials, and uniforms, is provided to deserving and underprivileged students to ensure equal access to quality education.

Centre for Speech and Language Therapy
The Centre for Speech and Language Therapy at Tanzeem-al-Lissan functions as a specialized clinical service unit primarily focused on assessment, diagnosis, and therapeutic intervention for individuals with speech, language, communication, and swallowing disorders.
Unlike a Department of Speech & Language Therapy, which usually operates within an academic institution and focuses on teaching, training, and research, this Centre is client-oriented and service-based. Its primary purpose is to provide direct clinical services to children and adults in a fully equipped therapeutic environment. While training and collaboration may take place, the Centre's core role is rehabilitation and therapy delivery, not academic instruction.
Mission
Our mission is to offer high-quality, accessible, and affordable speech and language therapy services in a specially equipped clinical setting, aimed at improving communication abilities and effectively addressing speech, language, and swallowing disorders.
Objectives
* To help individuals develop maximum functional ability to understand and use language for effective communication and self-expression.
* To provide skilled clinical services through comprehensive evaluation, diagnosis, and treatment of a wide range of communication disorders.
* To enhance speech, language, and communication skills that enable individuals to interact more effectively in daily life.
* To work in partnership with clients and their families to achieve positive and sustainable therapy outcomes.

Communication is fundamental to human interaction. It enables the exchange of ideas, thoughts, emotions, and information and plays a critical role in personal identity, learning, social interaction, and professional life. Difficulties in communication can significantly affect an individual's academic performance, social relationships, emotional well-being, and overall quality of life.
Recognizing the need for specialized intervention for individuals with communication disorders, Tanzeem-al-Lissan established the Centre for Speech and Language Therapy in 2016. The Centre was created to provide outstanding, evidence-based, and client-Centred clinical services to children and adults experiencing speech, language, and communication challenges.
The Centre offers both individual and group-based therapy interventions, depending on the age, diagnosis, severity of the condition, and specific needs of the client. Our Speech and Language Therapists work closely with parents, caregivers, teachers, and collaborate with medical and allied health professionals to ensure a holistic and coordinated approach to therapy.
We Deal With
The Centre provides assessment and therapy services for individuals with the following conditions:
* Hearing Impairment
* Children using Hearing Aids and Cochlear Implants
* Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)
* Language Disorders
* Specific Language Impairment (SLI)
* Speech and language delays due to developmental delays
* Articulation and Phonological Disorders
* Cleft Lip and Cleft Palate
* Tongue Tie (Ankyloglossia)
* Swallowing and Feeding Difficulties
* Stammering / Stuttering
* Other Speech and Communication Disorders
* Neurological speech and language disorders, including:
o Aphasia
o Apraxia
o Dysarthria
o Stroke-related communication disorders
o Traumatic brain injury


Therapeutic Approach
The Centre follows a structured and individualized therapy model that includes:
* Detailed case history and initial assessment
* Formal and informal speech and language evaluations
* Diagnosis and goal setting
* Individualized therapy planning
* Regular therapy sessions (individual or group-based)
* Family counselling and home-program guidance
* Continuous monitoring and periodic re-assessment
Each client receives a personalized intervention plan tailored to their specific communication needs and developmental level.
Collaborative Practice
Speech and Language Therapists at the Centre work in close coordination with:
* Parents and caregivers
* Teachers and special educators
* Audiologists
* Psychologists
* Physiotherapists and Occupational Therapists
* Medical professionals
This multidisciplinary collaboration ensures comprehensive management and improved therapy outcomes.

Institution of Functional Rehabilitation of Autism Spectrum Disorder (IFRA)
Introduction
Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a complex neurodevelopmental condition that affects communication, social interaction, behaviour, and functional skills. Families, educators, and service providers are often overwhelmed by the vast amount of information and the wide range of treatment options available for ASD, many of which are confusing or contradictory. Making informed, evidence-based decisions is therefore essential for the effective rehabilitation and development of children with autism.
To respond to this critical need, Tanzeem-al-Lissan established the Institution of Functional Rehab of Autism Spectrum Disorder (IFRA) - Centre for Autism Spectrum Disorder in 2014. The Centre is dedicated to providing scientifically validated, evidence-based, and child-Centred interventions for individuals with Autism Spectrum Disorder.
IFRA is recognized as one of the largest and leading providers of autism-specific interventions in Faisalabad, offering comprehensive services ranging from assessment and diagnosis to individualized treatment and long-term rehabilitation planning across the lifespan. Each child enrolled at IFRA receives highly individualized behavioural, educational, functional, and vocational programming based on their unique strengths, needs, and developmental profile.
Mission
To deliver quality education and therapy that develop the abilities of children with Autism Spectrum Disorder, fostering independence, self-reliance, and meaningful participation in family and community life 
Objectives
* To provide a comprehensive and individualized rehabilitation program that enables children with Autism Spectrum Disorder to achieve their maximum developmental potential while respecting individual differences.
* To design and implement structured educational programs that ensure continuous development of cognitive, communication, social, and functional skills.
* To promote social interaction, cooperation, and peer relationships among children with Autism Spectrum Disorder through structured group activities.
* To empower children with Autism Spectrum Disorder through quality education, skill-based training, and evidence-based interventions delivered by trained professionals and a collaborative multidisciplinary team.
* To provide a safe, welcoming, and nurturing environment where children and their families feel respected, valued, and supported.
* To support families of children with autism by offering parent training programs, counseling services, and psychological support, helping them cope with emotional stress and effectively participate in their child's rehabilitation process.
Evidence-Based Approach
IFRA is committed to the use of internationally recognized and evidence-based intervention models, ensuring effective and measurable outcomes. The Centre offers up-to-date services, including:
* Applied Behaviour Analysis (ABA)
* Assessment of Basic Language and Learning Skills (ABLLS)
* Early Intensive Behavioural Intervention (EIBI)
* Treatment and Education of Autistic and Communication-Handicapped Children (TEACCH)
* Picture Exchange Communication System (PECS)
These approaches are integrated into individualized treatment plans to support communication, behaviour regulation, learning, independence, and social participation.
Working Process at IFRA
The service delivery at IFRA follows a systematic and structured rehabilitation process, ensuring continuity of care and effective progress monitoring.
1. Assessment
A detailed multidisciplinary assessment is conducted to evaluate developmental levels, communication skills, behaviour, sensory needs, academic abilities, and functional independence.
2. Diagnosis
Based on assessment findings and clinical observations, a formal diagnosis is confirmed or supported, following recognized diagnostic guidelines.
3. Individualized Planning
Customized intervention plans are developed, outlining short-term and long-term goals tailored to the child's specific needs and abilities.

4. Therapeutic Sessions
Planned therapeutic and educational sessions are implemented using evidence-based strategies, with continuous monitoring of progress.
5. Re-assessment
Periodic reassessments are conducted to evaluate progress, identify new needs, and revise goals accordingly.
6. Follow-Up Sessions
Ongoing follow-up ensures continuity, maintenance of learned skills, and long-term developmental support.
Modes of Service Provision 
IFRA - Centre for Autism Spectrum Disorder follows a structured, child-Centreed, and evidence-based service delivery model to address the diverse developmental, educational, and therapeutic needs of children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). Services are planned individually while also promoting social participation through carefully designed group programs.
1. Individual Sessions
Individual sessions form the core mode of service provision at IFRA. These sessions are tailored to meet the unique needs, abilities, and developmental levels of each child.
* Each child undergoes a comprehensive assessment to identify strengths, challenges, and priority areas.
* Individualized goals are developed in areas such as communication, behaviour, social skills, academics, self-help, and sensory integration.
* Sessions are conducted on a one-to-one basis, allowing therapists to provide focused attention and immediate feedback.
* Interventions are regularly monitored and reviewed, and goals are updated according to the child's progress.
Purpose:
* To provide intensive and targeted intervention
* To address specific developmental delays or behavioural concerns
* To ensure measurable and consistent progress for each child
2. Group Sessions (Conducted Separately by Activity Type)
Group sessions are organized separately according to activity and skill area, ensuring that children are placed in groups that best match their developmental level and learning needs. These sessions promote socialization, cooperation, and real-life skill application in a structured and supportive environment.
a) Virtual Learning Groups
* Designed to support children in technology-assisted learning environments
* Focus on attention span, following instructions, digital interaction, and online classroom behaviour
* Helps children adapt to remote and blended learning models
b) Functional Skill Learning Groups
* Emphasis on daily living skills such as self-care, personal hygiene, time management, and basic community skills
* Activities are practical and hands-on to promote independence
* Encourages responsibility and confidence in everyday tasks
c) Educational Learning Groups
* Focus on academic readiness and learning skills including literacy, numeracy, cognitive development, and classroom behaviour
* Structured lessons with visual aids and interactive methods
* Supports school readiness and academic participation
d) Occupational Therapy Groups
* Address sensory processing, fine motor skills, gross motor skills, and body coordination
* Activities help improve handwriting, posture, balance, and sensory regulation
* Enhances participation in both academic and daily activities
e) Play Therapy Groups
* Use play as a therapeutic tool to develop social interaction, emotional expression, imagination, and problem-solving
* Encourages turn-taking, sharing, and joint attention
* Helps children's express feelings in a safe and enjoyable environment
f) Inclusive Education Groups
* Prepare children for mainstream or inclusive educational settings
* Focus on peer interaction, classroom routines, rule-following, and cooperative learning
* Promotes acceptance, social integration, and participation alongside peers
3. Family-Centred and Holistic Approach
IFRA strongly believes that family involvement is essential for the success of children with ASD.
Family Support Includes:
* Parent counselling and guidance sessions
* Training on behaviour management and skill reinforcement at home
* Regular progress updates and collaborative goal planning
* Emotional support and empowerment of caregivers



















Rehabilitation of Physically Challenged Children
Tanzeem-al-Lissan is a non-profit organization dedicated to the education and rehabilitation of children with special needs. As part of its commitment to improving the lives of physically challenged children, the Centre for Physically Challenged Children was established in 2002.
The Centre provides a safe, inclusive, and supportive environment where children receive quality education and rehabilitation services aimed at holistic development. The Physiotherapy Unit focuses on improving physical functioning while supporting cognitive, social, emotional, and adaptive skills necessary for independence and lifelong learning.
Each child receives services based on Individualized Education Plans (IEPs) and Individualized Treatment Plans (ITPs) to ensure appropriate interventions, continuous support, and measurable progress.
Vision

* To integrate physiotherapy with education for students up to the middle standard.
* To offer home-based physiotherapy services to non-affording and elderly patients.
* To deliver rehabilitation services for trauma, stroke, and other physical conditions through the Mobile Physiotherapy Unit.

Services Offered
The Physiotherapy Unit at the Centre for Physically Challenged Children is an integral component of our rehabilitation framework. It is designed to provide comprehensive, accessible, and high-quality physiotherapy services to children, adults, and elderly individuals with physical impairments. Our services focus on improving mobility, functional independence, and overall quality of life through evidence-based therapeutic interventions.
1. On-Campus Physiotherapy Services
The Centre provides structured physiotherapy services to enrolled children with physical disabilities. These services are delivered by qualified and experienced physiotherapists using individualized treatment plans based on detailed assessments.
Therapy focuses on:
* Improving gross and fine motor skills
* Enhancing muscle strength, flexibility, and endurance
* Correcting posture and movement patterns
* Improving balance, coordination, and gait
* Preventing secondary complications such as contractures and deformities
Each child receives individualized physiotherapy sessions aligned with their specific diagnosis and functional needs. Progress is continuously monitored, and treatment plans are adjusted accordingly to ensure optimal outcomes.

2. Educational Support up to Middle Standard
Alongside physiotherapy, the Centre offers educational services up to the middle standard for children with physical disabilities. This integrated approach ensures that children do not fall behind academically due to mobility limitations or prolonged medical needs.
Key features include:
* Adapted teaching methodologies suitable for physically challenged children
* Coordination between educators and physiotherapists to support functional learning
* Emphasis on independence, classroom participation, and daily living skills
* A supportive and accessible learning environment
This combined rehabilitation and education model enables children to progress academically while receiving continuous therapeutic support.

3. Home-Based Physiotherapy Services
(For Non-Affording and Elderly Patients)
To ensure equitable access to rehabilitation services, the Centre provides home-based physiotherapy to non-affording patients and elderly individuals who are unable to visit the Centre due to physical, financial, or logistical constraints.
These services are particularly designed for patients suffering from:
* Post-traumatic conditions
* Stroke (Cerebrovascular Accident)
* Age-related mobility issues
* Neurological and musculoskeletal disorders
Home-based physiotherapy allows patients to receive personalized care in the comfort of their homes, promoting faster recovery and reducing caregiver burden.

4. Mobile Physiotherapy Unit
The Mobile Physiotherapy Unit is a unique outreach initiative aimed at serving underprivileged communities and elderly patients with limited access to rehabilitation facilities.
Through this unit, trained physiotherapists provide on-site services to patients suffering from:
* Trauma-related injuries
* Stroke and post-stroke complications
* Chronic physical disabilities
* Other mobility and functional impairments
The Mobile Physiotherapy Unit ensures:
* Early intervention and continuity of care
* Reduced transportation challenges for patients
* Access to professional physiotherapy services for marginalized populations
This initiative reflects the Centre's commitment to inclusive healthcare and community-based rehabilitation.

Post Graduate Diploma in Autism Spectrum Disorder (PGD ASD)
Autism, also known as Autism Spectrum Disorder, is a group of disorders characterized by difficulties with social skills, repetitive behaviours, speech and nonverbal communication, as well as distinct strengths and distinctions. There isn't just one variety of autism; there are several, each caused by a unique combination of genetic and environmental factors. The term "spectrum" refers to the vast range of challenges and capabilities that each individual with autism possesses. The most visible indicators of autism usually show between the ages of 2 and 3. It can be diagnosed as early as 18 months in some cases. Some autism-related developmental deficits can be detected and addressed even sooner.
Autistic children and adults may benefit from individual or group attention/therapies from autism specialists. The Diploma in Autism will assist parents, caregivers, and professionals in early intervention, education, and therapy for children with autism that may result in considerable progress. Professionals can work in a variety of settings (schools, homes, and hospitals), giving them the opportunity to help young children and their families. Professionals will be better able to collaborate with a multidisciplinary group.
Vision
Empowering students to pursue their academic and professional goals through market-driven quality education in a diverse, welcoming, and inclusive environment.
Mission
To produce practitioners who provide professional services and support to individuals with autism spectrum disorder in home, school and community settings.
Objectives
The number of children with Autism is increasing, and there is a scarcity of professionals in this field. The start of a Diploma Course is required to address the demands of Special Education Departments, Regular Education Institutions, and parents of children with Autism. It will also give trained professionals who will support parents and meet the needs of people with autism. All those with the necessary academic background will be able to enroll in the diploma program and subsequently find work in both private and public sectors. The following are the key objectives of the diploma:
1. Equip successful graduates with sufficient knowledge, skills and expertise required to work with and to support of children with ASD.

Post Magisterial Diploma in Clinical Psychology  (PMDCP)
The Post Graduate Diploma in Clinical Psychology (PMDCP) is a specialized professional qualification designed to bridge the gap between theoretical psychology and clinical practice. It is particularly focused on equipping practitioners with the skills to diagnose, treat, and rehabilitate individuals with various psychological conditions.
Given your leadership at Danish College and your work with special children, these elements are often tailored to align with institutional goals of inclusion and professional excellence.
Vision
To produce professionally competent, ethically grounded, and compassionate clinical psychologists who contribute to the mental health and well-being of society through evidence-based practice and community service.
Mission
The mission of the PMDCP program is to:
* Provide rigorous clinical training that integrates psychological theories with practical diagnostic and therapeutic skills.
* Foster an environment of research and continuous learning to address the evolving mental health needs of diverse populations.
* Promote ethical standards and professionalism in the field of clinical psychology, especially in the context of rehabilitation and special education.
Objectives
The core objectives of the PMDCP program typically include:
1. Clinical Assessment: To train students in the administration, scoring, and interpretation of various psychological tests (intelligence, personality, and diagnostic tools).
2. Therapeutic Intervention: To develop proficiency in various counselling techniques and therapeutic modalities, such as Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT).
3. Diagnosis and Classification: To enable students to accurately diagnose psychological disorders using international classification systems like the DSM-5 or ICD-11.
4. Special Education Integration: To equip practitioners with the specialized skills needed to support children with developmental disabilities, such as Autism and ADHD.
5. Community Outreach: To prepare professionals who can provide mental health advocacy and support services to underprivileged or marginalized communities.
6. Professional Ethics: To instil a deep understanding of the legal and ethical responsibilities involved in clinical practice and patient confidentiality.

The Post Graduate Diploma in Clinical Psychology (PMDCP) is a premier one-year professional training program. It is specifically designed for graduates holding a Master's (MA/MSc) or a 4-year BS in Psychology who wish to specialize in the clinical field.
While a general degree provides the necessary theoretical foundation, the PMDCP at Danish College focuses on applied clinical skills. It serves as a vital bridge between academic study and professional practice, equipping graduates to serve as Clinical Psychologists in hospitals, private clinics, and specialized rehabilitation centres.
Core Components of the Program
The curriculum is structured to ensure that students transition from the classroom to the clinic with confidence:
* Psychodiagnosis: Mastery of standardized diagnostic tools (including the DSM-5-TR) and psychological testing to accurately assess mental, emotional, and behavioural disorders.
* Psychotherapy: Intensive training in evidence-based therapeutic modalities, such as Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) and Client-centred Therapy.
* Clinical Internship: A mandatory, hands-on practicum where students work under expert supervision in clinical settings, completing a required number of therapy hours and case reports.
* Clinical Research: Utilizing scientific methodologies to evaluate clinical cases and improve patient treatment outcomes.

With over three decades of dedicated service in mental health, Tanzeem-al-Lissan Faisalabad recognized the need to enhance the professional capacity of mental health practitioners in Pakistan and therefore initiated the PMDCP at Danish Degree College Special Education.The PMDCP was strategically launched to:
* Build a Skilled Workforce: Developing specialized human resources for the fields of special education and rehabilitation.
* Ensure Standardized Care: Providing professional training that aligns with modern, global clinical practices.
* Empower Caregivers: Enabling educators to deliver effective, child-centered interventions.
* Promote Inclusion: Supporting national efforts toward the independence and social participation of individuals with special needs.

Strategic Impact of the PMDCP
The launch of the PMDCP represents a significant milestone in our mission to serve the community through four key pillars:
1. Holistic Support for Special Children
We recognize that physical and educational support are only parts of the puzzle. Many children with special needs face co-occurring psychological challenges, such as trauma or anxiety. This program trains professionals who can provide specialized clinical interventions on-site.
2. Integrating Psychology and Special Education
By merging these disciplines, we empower professionals to:
* Accurately identify neurodevelopmental disorders like Autism and ADHD.
* Develop Individualized Education Plans (IEPs) that incorporate behavioural therapy.
* Provide essential mental health counselling for the families of special children.
3. Professional Advancement and Affiliation
Building on our successful history of affiliation with GC University Faisalabad (GCUF), the PMDCP offers a recognized pathway for graduates to gain professional certification. This qualification is vital for clinical roles within the Health Department and the Armed Forces (Army/Navy/Air Force).
4. Addressing the Mental Health Gap
There is an immense demand for qualified clinical psychologists in the Faisalabad region and across Pakistan. Danish College is committed to the social and economic betterment of our community by producing practitioners who are ready to serve in both the public and private sectors.
The official Scheme of Study for the Post Magistral Diploma in Clinical Psychology (PMDCP) at Government College University Faisalabad (GCUF) is designed to provide intensive clinical training over two semesters. The program consists of 34 total credit hours, distributed between core coursework and mandatory clinical field experience.

Associate Degree Program in Special Education (ADP Special Education) (2 Years Program)
Introduction
The Associate Degree in Education (ADE) program has been designed in line with UE vision to prepare academically, intellectually and socially vibrant educational leaders and teachers. This Program has all the elements and strengths of first 2-years (first 4 semesters) of the "Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.)".
The professional education needs lifetime commitment and intensive training (generally for four years such as the engineering and medical). Keeping the standard of professional education in view, a new ADE 2-year program (which is equivalent to first 2 years of the 4- Year "Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.)" program) is introduced and presented in the subsequent pages. This program offers in-depth knowledge, and intensive training in required professional skills to the prospective teachers, alongside the development of their professional attitude. This program fully appreciates the National Professional Standards for Teachers in Pakistan, and the world's best practices for the development of teacher to prepare the dynamic teachers for the coming times.
A classroom teacher needs to be competent in the content areas as well as in teaching strategies in order to ensure expected student learning outcomes. Hence, a blend of content and pedagogical courses has been provided in the scheme of study to prepare prospective teachers as professionals in education.
In addition, the program is based on the principal of choice and flexibility, as the program offers a range of Social and Natural Science disciplines, so that students may choose disciplines of their interest.
Program Vision
The vision of ADSE Program is to prepare dynamic leaders and practitioners in teaching, research and management havin content excellence, pedagogical competence, commitment and integrity who may ensure quality and sustainable development at all tiers and sectors of education.
Program Mission
To produce graduates who provide professional educational services to the individuals having Special Educational Needs in making them independent in their pastimes and social life.
Program Objectives
The objectives of the ADSE Program are in accordance with the university of Education goals and are focused to:
The program has the following primary objectives:
1. To enhance the standard of special educational services in Pakistan through producing educators, researchers, consultants, and other professionals who can promote the application of evidence-based practices to the service of individuals having special
educational needs.
2. To produce graduates who are equipped with knowledge, skills and expertise to work with individuals having special educational needs in both inclusive and special environment.
3. To prepare a new generation of special educationists whose central concern is to contribute the educational and emotional well-being of children and adolescents having special educational needs.
4. To produce graduates who can be productive in producing new knowledge in the field through research as well as in their academic and professional career.
